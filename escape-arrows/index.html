01 — The game

Three moves. One clear exit.

Most campaign boards are untimed. Late Boss and God levels add a clock — plan your route before it runs out.

01

Read the board.

Every arrow points one way. Look for the lanes that reach the edge without a blocker.

02

Find a clear path.

Long-press any arrow to preview its route before you commit to the move.

03

Slide every arrow out.

Tap a clear arrow to send it off the board. Clear them all to finish the level.

04 — Privacy, plainly

Your progress stays yours.

Escape Arrows is built to be played, not to collect. There is no account, no profile, and no document or file collection. Your levels, stars, settings, and streak are stored locally on your Android device.

The app is free and ad-supported. Google AdMob may process your advertising ID to serve and measure ads. If you opt into leaderboards, Google Play Games handles your player ID and scores.

05 — Good to know

Questions, answered.

Short answers to the things players and reviewers ask most. The full details live in the privacy policy.

Is it free?

Yes. Escape Arrows is free to play and supported by ads. There are no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without signing in. Google Play Games sign-in is optional and only used for leaderboards.

Where is my progress stored?

Locally on your device, in Android app storage. We do not keep a copy on our servers.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The puzzle itself works offline. Ads, leaderboards, and app updates need a connection.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

There are ads served by Google AdMob, including an optional rewarded ad for a revive. There are no in-app purchases.

Closed testing

Escape Arrows is in closed testing on Google Play. The public listing is not open yet.
