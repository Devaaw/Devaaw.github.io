Escape Arrows — Data Deletion Request

App: Escape Arrows (com.escapearrows.game) — Developer: Escape-Arrows

Escape Arrows is a single-player puzzle game. We do not operate any account system and we do not store your personal data on our own servers. The only data the app keeps is your game progress and settings, stored locally on your device. Because of that, deletion is immediate and fully under your control.

Data we keep locally on your device

  • Game progress: highest unlocked level, stars per level, best score per level, total score, hints used.
  • Daily challenge state (claimed date and bonus).
  • Hard mode state.
  • App settings: sound, music, vibration, display preferences.

How to delete all of this data (in-app / on-device)

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Go to Apps (or Applications) → Escape Arrows.
  3. Tap Storage → Clear data (Clear storage).

Alternative: uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data permanently.

Result: your progress, scores and settings are erased and the game starts fresh.

Advertising data

The app may show ads served by third-party ad networks (for example Google AdMob). When ads are shown, these networks may process a device advertising identifier to deliver ads and prevent fraud. This data is processed by the ad network, not by us, and is subject to their policies.

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID at any time from Settings → Google → Ads (Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID).
  • Opt out of personalised ads in the same Google Ads settings.

Leaderboards (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games to appear on the leaderboards, your Play Games profile (player ID and display name) and your submitted scores are handled by Google. You can stop this at any time by signing out of Google Play Games, and you can delete that data through your Google account settings at myaccount.google.com.
