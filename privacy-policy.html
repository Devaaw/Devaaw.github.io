Privacy Policy — Escape Arrows

Last updated: OCTOBER_2026 · App package: com.escapearrows.game · Developer: Escape-Arrows

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Android application Escape Arrows, distributed on Google Play under the package name com.escapearrows.game. It does not cover other websites or services we may link to.

2. Our general principle

The app is free, has no accounts and no in-app or external purchases. We do not collect directly identifying information (no name, email, phone number, geographic location, contacts, photos or files). We do not sell user data and we do not share it for marketing purposes.

3. Data we process

3.1 Data stored locally on your device only

The app keeps the following on your device using Android preferences storage. It is never uploaded to our servers and we hold no copy of it:

  • Game progress: highest unlocked level, stars per level, best score per level, total score, hints used.
  • Daily challenge: claim date and reward.
  • Hard mode state.
  • App settings: sound, music, vibration and display preferences.

Your control: you may delete all of this at any time from Settings → Apps → Escape Arrows → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

3.2 What we do not collect

We do not collect or use: name, email, phone number, geographic location, contacts, browsing history, personal files, or payment card data.

3.3 No payments

The app is completely free. It contains no purchasable in-game currency and no form of electronic commerce, and therefore we neither receive nor process any payment data.

4. Permissions and why

PermissionPurpose
INTERNETLoading ads when enabled, checking app updates from Google Play, and posting your scores to Google Play Games leaderboards when that feature is enabled.

We do not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or external storage permissions.

5. Third-party services

5.1 Advertising (Google AdMob / Unity Ads)

The app integrates third-party ad networks, and ads may be disabled or enabled depending on the app build. When ads are enabled, these networks may collect:

  • the advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and device/system identifiers;
  • device information (model, OS, language, connection);
  • ad interaction data (impressions/clicks) for performance measurement and fraud prevention.

This data is used by the network, not by us, for ad measurement, fraud prevention and personalised advertising where permitted.

Your controls:

  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • In the EEA/UK a consent request for advertising cookies is shown before personalised ads are enabled.
  • Similar prompts may appear in other regions.

Transparency note: in the current version ads are disabled, but the ad provider is bundled and may initialise itself; we therefore disclose this here and in the Play "Data safety" form.

5.2 Google Play Games (optional, off by default)

If you choose "Sign in with Google Play Games" from the leaderboard screen, the sign-in creates a connection with Google to display your name and rank on the leaderboards. Google then collects: player ID, display name, your progress and leaderboard scores. We only submit your scores to the leaderboards. You may skip this feature with no effect on gameplay. It is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5.3 App updates

The app uses the Google Play interface to update itself when a new version is available; only version information is used, with no personal data.

5.4 Android automatic backup

Android may back up app data to your own Google account according to your backup settings. This is not controlled by us. To disable it: Settings → System → Backup.

6. Data sharing

  • We never sell data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or marketing parties directly.
  • We do not share data with third parties other than those in section 5.
  • We may disclose data if required by a court order or legal obligation.
  • When leaderboards are enabled, your scores are processed by Google as a service provider to display rankings.

7. Retention

  • Local data: remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Advertising: retained by the networks under their own policies.
  • Leaderboard scores: retained while the feature is enabled.

8. Security

Your data is stored locally on your device under Android's security model. Transfers (ads, updates, leaderboards) use encrypted connections. No system is 100% secure, but we limit collection to what is necessary.

9. Children's privacy

The app is directed at a general audience (13+). We do not knowingly request or collect identifying information from children. If we learn that a child's data has been collected through an ad network or service, we will delete it promptly. Parents may contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Your rights

  • Access/correct/delete your local data by clearing app data.
  • Withdraw consent from Google Play Games by signing out.
  • Disable or reset the advertising ID in Google settings.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when functionality changes. Any material change will be announced in the app or on this page with an updated date. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
